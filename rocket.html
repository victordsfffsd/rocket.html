Freestyle scoring guide

Hold a dribble, manual, two-wheel ride or ceiling shuffle to build points. Sustained tricks count once toward the multiplier. Their rate increases during the first six seconds.

Link tricks before the meter empties. Recover upright for 0.65 seconds to bank. A goal can finish a ball combo. An uncontrolled roof landing loses the pending points. Training resets cancel the combo.

Repeated tricks earn fewer points. The first three of each trick can increase the multiplier, up to ×12. Dash, reset and pogo chains earn active-time points after two confirmed actions.

Zap Dash requires a front-wheel landing, a jump pop and a forward dash. The 180° and 360° dashes require a cancelled flip and a wheel-supported turn, then upright recovery.

Horse dashes score each completed back/front cycle and add time during repeated wheel-supported rocking. Short dashes score an early-cancelled flip into a front-wheel slide, then add time while the slide continues. Flat landings and unsupported gaps stop these timers. Both share dash repetition penalties.

Hel jump multi-dashes use a front-wheel jump pop and restored flip to start a ground dash chain. The first dash keeps its ordinary points; the second and later completed dashes earn 225 base points before shared dash repetition penalties. The chain adds time while moving with tire support, for up to half a second after the last completed dash.

Hel and Lix jumps score after taking off with an untimed flip restored by surface contact. A Lix jump replaces its wall-dash award. Amalix is the same scored action. Murie pops require the bouncing ball to lift the car's nose during landing, followed by takeoff with the flip retained. These are completed launches, without a continuing time rate.

Most flicks require an owned setup, a dodge and a ball launch. Musty flicks also work from aerial setups. Direction, rotation and setup select one flick award. Flick variants share repetition penalties, as do reset variants and dash variants. Half flips and speedflips score on upright recovery.

Classy flicks require a rolling turn into inversion before a backward launch. Suffo flicks require jumping away from a carried ball and returning with a diagonal backward flip. Each scores once when the ball launches.

JZR flicks require a backward diagonal launch with an immediate forward flip cancel. Directional air roll and diagonal stick inputs can produce the same award. A failed cancel keeps the ordinary flick's points if the ball was launched.

Mawkzy flicks require a carried-ball jump, a partial rolling turn, an early backward-diagonal flip cancel and a strike with the opposite front corner. One completed launch earns one flick award. There is no time rate; the goal bonus applies only after a goal.

Cabbage Muncher requires a ground carry, a turning jump into a stall reset, then a diagonal forward flick from one front tire. The reset scores immediately; the completed flick adds 300 base points. A failed finish keeps the reset under normal combo rules. The flick has no time rate and shares flick repetition penalties. Its fixed goal bonus applies only after a goal.

Bismillah flicks require a carried jump, a roll underneath the ball, then a cancelled diagonal front flip that scoops upward from a lower front corner. The ground-carry route earns 250 base points before repetition penalties. It has no time rate and gets its fixed bonus only after a goal.

Slingshot flicks require a carried jump-turn, cancelled backward flip and ground-wheel rebound that launches the ball from the roof. The finish earns 250 base points before repetition penalties. Slingshot shares this award. There is no timer; its goal bonus applies only after a goal.

Windmill flicks require a sideways stall-spin or an inverted backflip-cancel spin that lifts the ball. The successful strike earns 250 base points before flick repetition penalties. Spinning adds no time points. Its goal bonus applies only after a goal.

Athena flick labels a completed flick that hits the crossbar and rebounds into the field. The original flick keeps its points and repetition count. The label adds no points, multiplier or time. A subsequent unassisted goal removes the miss label.

Reverse Mustys require pitching backward through inversion, then backflipping into a rear-side scoop that launches the ball forward. The completed scoop scores once. A Reverse Musty can set up or finish a Musty Psycho.

Dash flicks require a ground dash, a controlled catch and a fresh jumping flick. A half-turn before the backward wavedash, or a completed 180° wheel pivot, selects the 180° wavedash flick. The dash keeps its movement points; the later ball launch gets one flick award. Waiting does not add flick points.

Turtle resets require a ball-wheel reset while your roof touches the floor. Turtle flicks use the automatic righting action to launch a caught ball. Ball control keeps the roof-down setup active; losing control restores normal bail rules. Repeated turtle resets earn time only during supported control.

Maktuf, Murie, Shay and Jakze resets use the approach, rotation and cancellation sequence to select one award at acquisition. A 360 Maktuf replaces the basic Maktuf award. These variants share reset repetition penalties; chain time still requires repeated acquisitions and continued ball control.

Heli resets require an inverted yaw spin, a stall and another ball reset while staying flat. Each completed acquisition scores once and shares reset repetition and chain time. Spinning without another acquisition cannot keep the timer running.

Murie Cancel adds directional air roll and sideways steering to a cancelled backflip, then catches another reset. Both the short inverted route and the full rotation score at acquisition, with a base of 275 before repetition penalties. Repeated acquisitions share the reset timer; spinning alone adds no time. The goal bonus applies only after a goal.

Jerry resets start with a steep nose-down front flip before touching the ball. An early strong cancel flattens the car into the next reset. Backwards Jerry uses a nose-up setup facing away from the ball and a backflip cancel. Each acquisition scores once and shares reset repetition and chain time.

Pop resets require using the acquired flip for a separate upward ball pop and cancelling the forward flip. The reset pays immediately; the completed pop upgrades that same entry to 225 points before repetition penalties, keeping a higher existing value. It adds no reset count or multiplier link and does not restart chain time.

Wavedash resets require partial wheel contact on the ball, a short directional dodge and full wheel contact that stops the flip and restores it. Each completed acquisition scores once. Repeated acquisitions share the reset timer, including time supported by the airborne ball. Gaps stop time scoring; a grounded or intercepted ball ends the series. A dash on the floor followed by a later ball reset is scored separately as Ground-dash reset.

Arsenal dash resets require a nose-up approach, a backward lean and a backflip against the ball that stops at another confirmed reset. The completed acquisition earns 250 base points before repetition penalties and replaces the ordinary wavedash-reset award. Repeated acquisitions share reset-chain time.

Double taps require your touch, a backboard rebound and your airborne follow-up. Ground and ceiling setups select the corresponding double. Sidewall and corner reads require a rebound followed by your shot toward a goal. A Psycho redirects a backboard rebound across the field toward the far goal; a corner rebound selects Corner Psycho. Free play accepts either end.

A ground-pinch or Musty setup selects its Psycho variant. A Musty on the final redirect also selects Musty Psycho. The earlier setup keeps its points; the final touch gets one Psycho award. These variants share repetition penalties and add a goal bonus only if the shot scores.

Pass redirects require a teammate's pass and an airborne reception toward a goal. Team pinches, pass redirects and Car Reset require another car; standard free play has one car.

Squishy saves require driving through the goal onto its back wall and roof, then flipping out to clear an incoming goal-bound shot. Zen touches require a rolling upper-front catch that absorbs the ball's speed and maintains close aerial control for 0.3 seconds. Each completed save or catch scores once.

Pogos require a chassis rebound, a nearby ball bounce and continued ball control. Repeated pogos earn time while you stay airborne near the ball, for up to half a second after each cycle.

Ball tricks earn base points when completed, including pinches and unused flip resets. A goal within 12 seconds adds the listed bonus for each eligible trick. Your follow-up touches retain eligibility; another car taking over ends it. Missing the goal keeps the base points under the normal combo rules. A pinch goal without a later ground touch adds 50% to its goal bonus. The formation contact is allowed. Timed holds have a fixed goal bonus.

Purple Shot requires a confirmed reset, a separate backflip launch from the front-wheel area and early cancellation. It upgrades that reset to 275 base points, retaining any higher acquisition value. It does not add another reset or multiplier link. A single shot has no time rate; only repeated reset acquisitions can sustain reset-chain time.

      Freestyle scoring guide

      Hold a dribble, manual, two-wheel ride or ceiling shuffle to build points. Sustained tricks count once toward the multiplier. Their rate increases during the first six seconds.

      Link tricks before the meter empties. Recover upright for 0.65 seconds to bank. A goal can finish a ball combo. An uncontrolled roof landing loses the pending points. Training resets cancel the combo.

      Repeated tricks earn fewer points. The first three of each trick can increase the multiplier, up to ×12. Dash, reset and pogo chains earn active-time points after two confirmed actions.

      Zap Dash requires a front-wheel landing, a jump pop and a forward dash. The 180° and 360° dashes require a cancelled flip and a wheel-supported turn, then upright recovery.

      Horse dashes score each completed back/front cycle and add time during repeated wheel-supported rocking. Short dashes score an early-cancelled flip into a front-wheel slide, then add time while the slide continues. Flat landings and unsupported gaps stop these timers. Both share dash repetition penalties.

      Hel jump multi-dashes use a front-wheel jump pop and restored flip to start a ground dash chain. The first dash keeps its ordinary points; the second and later completed dashes earn 225 base points before shared dash repetition penalties. The chain adds time while moving with tire support, for up to half a second after the last completed dash.

      Hel and Lix jumps score after taking off with an untimed flip restored by surface contact. A Lix jump replaces its wall-dash award. Amalix is the same scored action. Murie pops require the bouncing ball to lift the car's nose during landing, followed by takeoff with the flip retained. These are completed launches, without a continuing time rate.

      Most flicks require an owned setup, a dodge and a ball launch. Musty flicks also work from aerial setups. Direction, rotation and setup select one flick award. Flick variants share repetition penalties, as do reset variants and dash variants. Half flips and speedflips score on upright recovery.

      Classy flicks require a rolling turn into inversion before a backward launch. Suffo flicks require jumping away from a carried ball and returning with a diagonal backward flip. Each scores once when the ball launches.

      JZR flicks require a backward diagonal launch with an immediate forward flip cancel. Directional air roll and diagonal stick inputs can produce the same award. A failed cancel keeps the ordinary flick's points if the ball was launched.

      Mawkzy flicks require a carried-ball jump, a partial rolling turn, an early backward-diagonal flip cancel and a strike with the opposite front corner. One completed launch earns one flick award. There is no time rate; the goal bonus applies only after a goal.

      Cabbage Muncher requires a ground carry, a turning jump into a stall reset, then a diagonal forward flick from one front tire. The reset scores immediately; the completed flick adds 300 base points. A failed finish keeps the reset under normal combo rules. The flick has no time rate and shares flick repetition penalties. Its fixed goal bonus applies only after a goal.

      Bismillah flicks require a carried jump, a roll underneath the ball, then a cancelled diagonal front flip that scoops upward from a lower front corner. The ground-carry route earns 250 base points before repetition penalties. It has no time rate and gets its fixed bonus only after a goal.

      Slingshot flicks require a carried jump-turn, cancelled backward flip and ground-wheel rebound that launches the ball from the roof. The finish earns 250 base points before repetition penalties. Slingshot shares this award. There is no timer; its goal bonus applies only after a goal.

      Windmill flicks require a sideways stall-spin or an inverted backflip-cancel spin that lifts the ball. The successful strike earns 250 base points before flick repetition penalties. Spinning adds no time points. Its goal bonus applies only after a goal.

      Athena flick labels a completed flick that hits the crossbar and rebounds into the field. The original flick keeps its points and repetition count. The label adds no points, multiplier or time. A subsequent unassisted goal removes the miss label.

      Reverse Mustys require pitching backward through inversion, then backflipping into a rear-side scoop that launches the ball forward. The completed scoop scores once. A Reverse Musty can set up or finish a Musty Psycho.

      Dash flicks require a ground dash, a controlled catch and a fresh jumping flick. A half-turn before the backward wavedash, or a completed 180° wheel pivot, selects the 180° wavedash flick. The dash keeps its movement points; the later ball launch gets one flick award. Waiting does not add flick points.

      Turtle resets require a ball-wheel reset while your roof touches the floor. Turtle flicks use the automatic righting action to launch a caught ball. Ball control keeps the roof-down setup active; losing control restores normal bail rules. Repeated turtle resets earn time only during supported control.

      Maktuf, Murie, Shay and Jakze resets use the approach, rotation and cancellation sequence to select one award at acquisition. A 360 Maktuf replaces the basic Maktuf award. These variants share reset repetition penalties; chain time still requires repeated acquisitions and continued ball control.

      Heli resets require an inverted yaw spin, a stall and another ball reset while staying flat. Each completed acquisition scores once and shares reset repetition and chain time. Spinning without another acquisition cannot keep the timer running.

      Murie Cancel adds directional air roll and sideways steering to a cancelled backflip, then catches another reset. Both the short inverted route and the full rotation score at acquisition, with a base of 275 before repetition penalties. Repeated acquisitions share the reset timer; spinning alone adds no time. The goal bonus applies only after a goal.

      Jerry resets start with a steep nose-down front flip before touching the ball. An early strong cancel flattens the car into the next reset. Backwards Jerry uses a nose-up setup facing away from the ball and a backflip cancel. Each acquisition scores once and shares reset repetition and chain time.

      Pop resets require using the acquired flip for a separate upward ball pop and cancelling the forward flip. The reset pays immediately; the completed pop upgrades that same entry to 225 points before repetition penalties, keeping a higher existing value. It adds no reset count or multiplier link and does not restart chain time.

      Wavedash resets require partial wheel contact on the ball, a short directional dodge and full wheel contact that stops the flip and restores it. Each completed acquisition scores once. Repeated acquisitions share the reset timer, including time supported by the airborne ball. Gaps stop time scoring; a grounded or intercepted ball ends the series. A dash on the floor followed by a later ball reset is scored separately as Ground-dash reset.

      Arsenal dash resets require a nose-up approach, a backward lean and a backflip against the ball that stops at another confirmed reset. The completed acquisition earns 250 base points before repetition penalties and replaces the ordinary wavedash-reset award. Repeated acquisitions share reset-chain time.

      Double taps require your touch, a backboard rebound and your airborne follow-up. Ground and ceiling setups select the corresponding double. Sidewall and corner reads require a rebound followed by your shot toward a goal. A Psycho redirects a backboard rebound across the field toward the far goal; a corner rebound selects Corner Psycho. Free play accepts either end.

      A ground-pinch or Musty setup selects its Psycho variant. A Musty on the final redirect also selects Musty Psycho. The earlier setup keeps its points; the final touch gets one Psycho award. These variants share repetition penalties and add a goal bonus only if the shot scores.

      Pass redirects require a teammate's pass and an airborne reception toward a goal. Team pinches, pass redirects and Car Reset require another car; standard free play has one car.

      Squishy saves require driving through the goal onto its back wall and roof, then flipping out to clear an incoming goal-bound shot. Zen touches require a rolling upper-front catch that absorbs the ball's speed and maintains close aerial control for 0.3 seconds. Each completed save or catch scores once.

      Pogos require a chassis rebound, a nearby ball bounce and continued ball control. Repeated pogos earn time while you stay airborne near the ball, for up to half a second after each cycle.

      Ball tricks earn base points when completed, including pinches and unused flip resets. A goal within 12 seconds adds the listed bonus for each eligible trick. Your follow-up touches retain eligibility; another car taking over ends it. Missing the goal keeps the base points under the normal combo rules. A pinch goal without a later ground touch adds 50% to its goal bonus. The formation contact is allowed. Timed holds have a fixed goal bonus.

      Purple Shot requires a confirmed reset, a separate backflip launch from the front-wheel area and early cancellation. It upgrades that reset to 275 base points, retaining any higher acquisition value. It does not add another reset or multiplier link. A single shot has no time rate; only repeated reset acquisitions can sustain reset-chain time.

          Freestyle scoring guide

          Hold a dribble, manual, two-wheel ride or ceiling shuffle to build points. Sustained tricks count once toward the multiplier. Their rate increases during the first six seconds.

          Link tricks before the meter empties. Recover upright for 0.65 seconds to bank. A goal can finish a ball combo. An uncontrolled roof landing loses the pending points. Training resets cancel the combo.

          Repeated tricks earn fewer points. The first three of each trick can increase the multiplier, up to ×12. Dash, reset and pogo chains earn active-time points after two confirmed actions.

          Zap Dash requires a front-wheel landing, a jump pop and a forward dash. The 180° and 360° dashes require a cancelled flip and a wheel-supported turn, then upright recovery.

          Horse dashes score each completed back/front cycle and add time during repeated wheel-supported rocking. Short dashes score an early-cancelled flip into a front-wheel slide, then add time while the slide continues. Flat landings and unsupported gaps stop these timers. Both share dash repetition penalties.

          Hel jump multi-dashes use a front-wheel jump pop and restored flip to start a ground dash chain. The first dash keeps its ordinary points; the second and later completed dashes earn 225 base points before shared dash repetition penalties. The chain adds time while moving with tire support, for up to half a second after the last completed dash.

          Hel and Lix jumps score after taking off with an untimed flip restored by surface contact. A Lix jump replaces its wall-dash award. Amalix is the same scored action. Murie pops require the bouncing ball to lift the car's nose during landing, followed by takeoff with the flip retained. These are completed launches, without a continuing time rate.

          Most flicks require an owned setup, a dodge and a ball launch. Musty flicks also work from aerial setups. Direction, rotation and setup select one flick award. Flick variants share repetition penalties, as do reset variants and dash variants. Half flips and speedflips score on upright recovery.

          Classy flicks require a rolling turn into inversion before a backward launch. Suffo flicks require jumping away from a carried ball and returning with a diagonal backward flip. Each scores once when the ball launches.

          JZR flicks require a backward diagonal launch with an immediate forward flip cancel. Directional air roll and diagonal stick inputs can produce the same award. A failed cancel keeps the ordinary flick's points if the ball was launched.

          Mawkzy flicks require a carried-ball jump, a partial rolling turn, an early backward-diagonal flip cancel and a strike with the opposite front corner. One completed launch earns one flick award. There is no time rate; the goal bonus applies only after a goal.

          Cabbage Muncher requires a ground carry, a turning jump into a stall reset, then a diagonal forward flick from one front tire. The reset scores immediately; the completed flick adds 300 base points. A failed finish keeps the reset under normal combo rules. The flick has no time rate and shares flick repetition penalties. Its fixed goal bonus applies only after a goal.

          Bismillah flicks require a carried jump, a roll underneath the ball, then a cancelled diagonal front flip that scoops upward from a lower front corner. The ground-carry route earns 250 base points before repetition penalties. It has no time rate and gets its fixed bonus only after a goal.

          Slingshot flicks require a carried jump-turn, cancelled backward flip and ground-wheel rebound that launches the ball from the roof. The finish earns 250 base points before repetition penalties. Slingshot shares this award. There is no timer; its goal bonus applies only after a goal.

          Windmill flicks require a sideways stall-spin or an inverted backflip-cancel spin that lifts the ball. The successful strike earns 250 base points before flick repetition penalties. Spinning adds no time points. Its goal bonus applies only after a goal.

          Athena flick labels a completed flick that hits the crossbar and rebounds into the field. The original flick keeps its points and repetition count. The label adds no points, multiplier or time. A subsequent unassisted goal removes the miss label.

          Reverse Mustys require pitching backward through inversion, then backflipping into a rear-side scoop that launches the ball forward. The completed scoop scores once. A Reverse Musty can set up or finish a Musty Psycho.

          Dash flicks require a ground dash, a controlled catch and a fresh jumping flick. A half-turn before the backward wavedash, or a completed 180° wheel pivot, selects the 180° wavedash flick. The dash keeps its movement points; the later ball launch gets one flick award. Waiting does not add flick points.

          Turtle resets require a ball-wheel reset while your roof touches the floor. Turtle flicks use the automatic righting action to launch a caught ball. Ball control keeps the roof-down setup active; losing control restores normal bail rules. Repeated turtle resets earn time only during supported control.

          Maktuf, Murie, Shay and Jakze resets use the approach, rotation and cancellation sequence to select one award at acquisition. A 360 Maktuf replaces the basic Maktuf award. These variants share reset repetition penalties; chain time still requires repeated acquisitions and continued ball control.

          Heli resets require an inverted yaw spin, a stall and another ball reset while staying flat. Each completed acquisition scores once and shares reset repetition and chain time. Spinning without another acquisition cannot keep the timer running.

          Murie Cancel adds directional air roll and sideways steering to a cancelled backflip, then catches another reset. Both the short inverted route and the full rotation score at acquisition, with a base of 275 before repetition penalties. Repeated acquisitions share the reset timer; spinning alone adds no time. The goal bonus applies only after a goal.

          Jerry resets start with a steep nose-down front flip before touching the ball. An early strong cancel flattens the car into the next reset. Backwards Jerry uses a nose-up setup facing away from the ball and a backflip cancel. Each acquisition scores once and shares reset repetition and chain time.

          Pop resets require using the acquired flip for a separate upward ball pop and cancelling the forward flip. The reset pays immediately; the completed pop upgrades that same entry to 225 points before repetition penalties, keeping a higher existing value. It adds no reset count or multiplier link and does not restart chain time.

          Wavedash resets require partial wheel contact on the ball, a short directional dodge and full wheel contact that stops the flip and restores it. Each completed acquisition scores once. Repeated acquisitions share the reset timer, including time supported by the airborne ball. Gaps stop time scoring; a grounded or intercepted ball ends the series. A dash on the floor followed by a later ball reset is scored separately as Ground-dash reset.

          Arsenal dash resets require a nose-up approach, a backward lean and a backflip against the ball that stops at another confirmed reset. The completed acquisition earns 250 base points before repetition penalties and replaces the ordinary wavedash-reset award. Repeated acquisitions share reset-chain time.

          Double taps require your touch, a backboard rebound and your airborne follow-up. Ground and ceiling setups select the corresponding double. Sidewall and corner reads require a rebound followed by your shot toward a goal. A Psycho redirects a backboard rebound across the field toward the far goal; a corner rebound selects Corner Psycho. Free play accepts either end.

          A ground-pinch or Musty setup selects its Psycho variant. A Musty on the final redirect also selects Musty Psycho. The earlier setup keeps its points; the final touch gets one Psycho award. These variants share repetition penalties and add a goal bonus only if the shot scores.

          Pass redirects require a teammate's pass and an airborne reception toward a goal. Team pinches, pass redirects and Car Reset require another car; standard free play has one car.

          Squishy saves require driving through the goal onto its back wall and roof, then flipping out to clear an incoming goal-bound shot. Zen touches require a rolling upper-front catch that absorbs the ball's speed and maintains close aerial control for 0.3 seconds. Each completed save or catch scores once.

          Pogos require a chassis rebound, a nearby ball bounce and continued ball control. Repeated pogos earn time while you stay airborne near the ball, for up to half a second after each cycle.

          Ball tricks earn base points when completed, including pinches and unused flip resets. A goal within 12 seconds adds the listed bonus for each eligible trick. Your follow-up touches retain eligibility; another car taking over ends it. Missing the goal keeps the base points under the normal combo rules. A pinch goal without a later ground touch adds 50% to its goal bonus. The formation contact is allowed. Timed holds have a fixed goal bonus.

          Purple Shot requires a confirmed reset, a separate backflip launch from the front-wheel area and early cancellation. It upgrades that reset to 275 base points, retaining any higher acquisition value. It does not add another reset or multiplier link. A single shot has no time rate; only repeated reset acquisitions can sustain reset-chain time.
